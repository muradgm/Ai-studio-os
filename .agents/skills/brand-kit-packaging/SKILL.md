# brand-kit-packaging

## Purpose
Turn an approved identity system into a traceable delivery package whose contents, versions, rights, provenance, and Brand DNA inheritance are explicit.

## When to use
- After the identity direction and required production assets have been reviewed.
- When preparing a client handoff, downloadable brand kit, or reusable project identity package.

## Inputs required
- Approved Brand DNA version.
- Produced artifact references and versions.
- Approval/review evidence.
- Rights/license metadata where relevant.
- Required delivery scope and representative applications.

## Operating principles
- Packaging never substitutes for production.
- Do not list an asset that does not actually exist.
- Every required artifact must inherit the same Brand DNA version.
- Preserve source/provenance and rights information.
- Font-family references and license metadata belong in the kit; font binaries do not unless redistribution rights explicitly permit them.
- Keep unresolved trademark/legal status visible.

## Workflow
1. Read the approved Brand DNA and required-kit scope.
2. Inventory produced strategy, direction, logo, color, typography, icon, guidelines, image/motion/voice and application artifacts.
3. Reject placeholders, promised future files, and missing artifact references.
4. Verify version inheritance and approval state.
5. Attach review, integrity, provenance, and rights evidence.
6. Build the canonical Brand Kit manifest.
7. Generate a deterministic destination plan for the deliverable folders.
8. Hand the manifest to independent `brand-kit-review` before delivery.

## Deliverables
- Brand Kit manifest.
- Artifact inventory with versions and Brand DNA inheritance.
- Rights/provenance inventory.
- Delivery folder plan.
- Explicit unresolved-risk list.

## Review criteria
- Every listed artifact exists as a concrete reference.
- Required categories are present and approved.
- No cross-version identity drift is hidden.
- Rights and legal uncertainty are represented accurately.
- Package structure is practical for downstream use.

## Failure modes
- Calling a moodboard a brand kit.
- Inventing files to make the package look complete.
- Shipping logo/icon assets from different identity versions.
- Redistributing fonts without rights.
- Hiding unresolved trademark or licensing questions.

## Handoffs
- `brand-kit-review` independently evaluates completeness and coherence.
- Delivery/export adapters may materialize the approved manifest into actual files or archives.
