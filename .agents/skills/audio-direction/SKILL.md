# audio-direction

## Procedure
1. Derive audio personality from creative direction and scene purpose.
2. Assign sound-design cues to valid storyboard beats.
3. Require concrete music-rights evidence for commercial use.
4. Prioritize intelligibility when voice is present.
5. Require captions and transcript for spoken content.
6. Avoid music or sound design that overstates emotion or competes with the visual narrative.
