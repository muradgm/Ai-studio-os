# logo-system-recipe

## Purpose
Orchestrate the complete logo-system workflow from brand strategy to locked canonical vector assets without confusing concept generation with production approval.

## When to use
- New logo systems.
- Major logo redesign/refinement.
- When a concept must become a reliable multi-format identity asset.

## Inputs required
- Approved brand positioning and desired perceptions.
- Inspiration/category research.
- Logo psychology hypotheses.
- Required applications, smallest size, print/digital constraints.
- Existing mark/assets if redesigning.

## Operating principles
- Assess all seven logo types before shortlisting.
- Diverge into distinct concept families before refinement.
- Black/white and small-size behavior precede color polish.
- Generated raster imagery is concept material, never the final master.
- The final source of truth is the approved canonical mark specification plus canonical SVG hash.
- Exact coordinate/Bézier/layer construction belongs to `vector-geometry-engineer` after concept approval.
- Vector construction must pass `vector-geometry-review` before artifact locks.
- Maker and reviewer remain independent.
- Trademark/confusion screening is not legal clearance.

## Workflow
1. `brand-strategist` defines positioning, category tension, memory objective, and anti-clichés.
2. Logo inspiration/psychology modules establish evidence-aware reference and perception hypotheses.
3. `logo-designer` assesses all seven logo types.
4. `logo-exploration` creates 3–5 concept families with variants, memory hooks, and kill criteria.
5. `logo-review`, `brand-fit-review`, and high-risk `creative-skeptic` compare surviving families.
6. User/Council explicitly selects a direction; if none is good enough, return to exploration.
7. `vector-geometry-engineer` reconstructs the approved finalist from an explicit geometry spec: viewBox/frame, stable shape IDs, coordinates, Bézier handles, optical center/corrections, layer z-order, overlaps, masks/clips, and target-size rules.
8. `vector-geometry-review` audits geometry, curves, corners, optical balance, small-size behavior, layer/paint order, and export readiness. Revise until approved.
9. Build primary, secondary, symbol, micro mark, wordmark, and favicon variants from the approved construction grammar.
10. Stress-test monochrome, inverse, 16/32/64/128px, print, signage, social/app icon, stamp/embroidery-like reproduction.
11. Approve canonical geometry, then run Shape / SVG / Layer / Overlap / Render integrity locks.
12. Package assets, construction/specification, clear-space/min-size rules, motion principle, and unresolved legal risks.

## Deliverables
- Type assessment.
- Concept-family exploration and Council/review verdict.
- Approved geometry spec and vector finalist.
- Independent vector-geometry review.
- Responsive logo system.
- Reproduction/stress-test evidence.
- Canonical mark specification and integrity evidence.
- Export package and usage notes.

## Review criteria
- Strategic fit and distinctive silhouette.
- Small-size/reproduction resilience.
- Optical and typographic quality.
- Vector geometry is reproducible from an explicit spec.
- Responsive variants remain recognizably one system.
- No unexplained geometry/color/layer/overlap drift.
- Originality risk has been screened and legal uncertainty labeled correctly.

## Failure modes
- Selecting from one family disguised as three concepts.
- Freezing image-generator geometry as the master.
- Sending an unapproved concept to exact vector construction.
- Color/gradient hiding a weak monochrome mark.
- Creating micro variants by blind scaling.
- Regenerating a locked logo and accepting drift.
- Calling preliminary similarity checks trademark clearance.

## Handoffs
- `vector-geometry-engineer` owns deterministic construction after concept selection.
- `vector-geometry-review` owns independent construction QA before locking.
- `logo-integrity` owns deterministic artifact locks after approval.
- `motion-designer` can derive motion from the mark's stable structural logic and supplied anchors/layers.
- `brand-identity-recipe` integrates the logo into the wider identity system.
