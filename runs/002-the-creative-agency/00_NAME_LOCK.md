# The Creative Agency — Name Lock

**Decision:** LOCKED as the working/public-facing product name for this app.

**Engine/runtime name:** AI Studio OS v1.2 remains the underlying technical system and repository identity.

## Naming state

- Product display name: **The Creative Agency**
- Short internal reference: **TCA** only in engineering/project metadata; do not make TCA the primary public wordmark by default.
- Naming intent: the app should behave like an integrated multidisciplinary creative agency rather than expose its internal OS architecture to clients.
- Commercial exclusivity: **UNRESOLVED / HIGH COLLISION TERRITORY**. Existing active businesses use the exact phrase “The Creative Agency.” This does not block internal testing, but external commercialization requires a dedicated naming/trademark/domain clearance gate.

## Brand rule

Do not compensate for the generic/descriptive name with generic agency aesthetics. Distinctiveness must come from the product experience, proprietary identity system, authored visual language, and evidence of how the agency works.
