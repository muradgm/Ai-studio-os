# brand-identity-kit-recipe

## Purpose
Produce a complete, versioned brand identity kit from one governing Brand DNA contract while reusing the existing logo and icon-system subsystems rather than collapsing everything into one generation step.

## When to use
- A project needs a full production identity kit rather than identity direction alone.
- Deliverables include a logo system, personalized icon family, typography/color rules, guidelines and application assets.
- A rebrand must be packaged for downstream product, web, campaign or motion teams.

## Inputs required
- Verified business/product truth.
- Audience, category context and positioning constraints.
- Existing identity/name/assets where relevant.
- Required applications and delivery scope.
- Rights/license constraints and known trademark/legal state.
- Explicit human/Council approval points for identity territory and canonical assets.

## Operating principles
- `brand-identity-recipe` owns identity formation; this recipe owns full-system production and delivery.
- Brand DNA is the shared contract above every downstream artifact.
- Use phase-specific routing; do not invoke every specialist simultaneously.
- Logo work delegates to `logo-system-recipe` and existing Logo Integrity locks.
- Personalized icon work delegates to `icon-system-recipe`, with 5–8 hard calibration icons before scaling.
- Icons must inherit the brand's geometry/visual language and may not be stock icons with cosmetic recoloring.
- Type/color/image/motion/voice systems must inherit Brand DNA rather than form independent mini-brands.
- Representative applications must test the identity before guidelines are frozen.
- Packaging includes only produced artifacts with real references.
- Font binaries, trademark clearance, customer proof, and rights are never invented.

## Workflow
1. **Truth + strategy** — `brand-strategist` defines positioning, audience, promise, category tension and constraints.
2. **Reference territory** — inspiration plus TAKE / REJECT / TRANSFORM and anti-references.
3. **Creative direction** — `art-direction` establishes one governing creative idea and concrete visual principles.
4. **Brand DNA draft** — encode strategy, voice, color, typography, geometry, imagery, motion and unresolved constraints into a versioned contract.
5. **Identity territory selection** — diverge, challenge and obtain explicit approval before canonical production.
6. **Logo subsystem** — route `logo-system-recipe`; construct/review/freeze approved masters through vector geometry and Logo Integrity.
7. **Personalized icon subsystem** — route `icon-system-recipe`; derive Icon DNA from Brand DNA, build 5–8 difficult calibration icons, independently review geometry, then scale the family.
8. **System production** — complete typography, color, graphic, imagery, voice/copy and motion rules required by the brief.
9. **Application proof** — test at least two representative real-world applications; refine surgically where the system fails.
10. **Independent system review** — `brand-fit-review`, relevant asset reviewers, then `brand-kit-review` evaluate coherence and delivery completeness.
11. **Brand DNA freeze** — freeze the approved DNA version only after downstream artifacts agree with it.
12. **Package** — `brand-kit-packaging` creates the versioned manifest, rights/provenance record and delivery plan.
13. **Deliver** — export adapters materialize only approved referenced artifacts; unresolved legal/licensing items remain visible.

## Deliverables
- Brand strategy and positioning.
- Approved Brand DNA contract.
- Creative/visual direction.
- Canonical logo system and integrity evidence.
- Personalized Icon DNA, calibration family and production SVG set.
- Typography and color systems.
- Graphic, imagery, motion and voice rules as required.
- Representative application assets.
- Brand guidelines artifact.
- Independent review evidence.
- Versioned Brand Kit manifest and delivery plan.

## Review criteria
- One strategic/creative idea survives across logo, icons, type, color, imagery, motion and voice.
- Every required artifact exists and targets the same Brand DNA version.
- Personalized icons demonstrate a recognizable family grammar and pass vector review.
- Logo masters pass the existing integrity locks.
- Guidelines are usable production rules, not only presentation slides.
- Representative applications prove the system outside an idealized brand board.
- Rights and legal status are accurately represented.

## Failure modes
- Running the entire specialist catalog at once.
- Generating logo, palette and icons independently and calling the result a system.
- Scaling an icon family before calibration is approved.
- Recoloring stock icons and labeling them personalized.
- Packaging placeholders or planned future files as deliverables.
- Shipping fonts without redistribution rights.
- Claiming trademark clearance without evidence.
- Freezing Brand DNA before representative applications expose weaknesses.

## Handoffs
- Approved kit becomes shared immutable context for product, web, campaign, motion, image and copy recipes until a versioned identity change is approved.
- Asset-specific failures route back to the responsible subsystem; do not regenerate the entire kit by default.
- Observation may propose future Brand DNA changes, but only validated changes create a new version.
