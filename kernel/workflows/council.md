# Council protocol

## Stage 1 — Frame
Define the exact decision, constraints, evidence, and success criteria.

## Stage 2 — Independent reviews
Each selected member analyzes without seeing other members' conclusions.

Each review must contain:
- recommendation
- strongest evidence
- assumptions
- risks
- confidence

## Stage 3 — Cross-critique
Each member identifies:
- one conclusion they strongly support
- one they reject
- one hidden assumption
- one overlooked risk
- one stronger alternative

## Stage 4 — Challenge
A challenger attempts to falsify the leading recommendation.

## Stage 5 — Rebuttal
The leading recommendation is defended only against valid challenges.

## Stage 6 — Chair
The chair does not average opinions. It resolves conflicts using evidence and constraints. Preserve unresolved dissent.

Final output:
- recommendation
- why
- strongest objection
- unresolved dissent
- assumptions requiring validation
- kill criteria
- confidence
- next action
