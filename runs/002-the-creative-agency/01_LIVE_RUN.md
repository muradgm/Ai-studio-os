# Live Run — Project 001

## Intent

Build the launch-ready creative and product direction for **The Creative Agency**, an AI-native multidisciplinary creative agency application powered internally by AI Studio OS v1.2.

## Product promise

A client brings a problem, brief, reference, asset, or incomplete idea. The agency researches, challenges, explores, creates, reviews, produces, and learns—using specialist runtimes rather than one-shot generation.

## Primary users

1. Founder / small business owner needing high-quality creative work without managing many specialists.
2. Product/brand team needing a second creative and technical team on demand.
3. Independent creative/operator wanting strategy, design, image, motion, video, writing, and implementation in one controlled workflow.

## Core jobs

- brand and identity
- websites / digital products
- logo systems
- image direction and retouch
- motion / interactive work
- video / audio / voice
- writing / messaging
- engineering implementation
- critique / review / red-team

## Product model

The client should not see a wall of AI models. The interface is organized around the work:

`Brief → Research → Inspiration → Explore → Council → Create → Review → Deliver → Learn`

Models/tools are adapters selected behind the scenes by the Creative Tool Gateway.

## Strategic differentiator

**Agency-grade process with AI-native speed, but with explicit human judgment gates and artifact integrity.**

The app must not market itself as “AI that makes everything.” Its strongest position is: **a governed creative team that can think and make across disciplines.**

## Anti-positioning

Reject:
- prompt box as the whole product
- model marketplace as the primary UX
- “10x creativity” claims
- magical sparkle/AI branding
- fake agency personas that merely restate one LLM answer
- infinite concept generation without selection
- outputs that cannot explain evidence, source assets, revisions, or approval state

## Success condition for this run

The run may advance to visual/product execution only if positioning, creative calibration, identity direction, product architecture, and production priorities remain coherent under Council review.
