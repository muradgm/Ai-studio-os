# Creative Knowledge Generalist Corpus V1 Charter

**Status:** Pre-registered design. This document defines the population protocol for a future general-purpose corpus. It does not create a knowledge library, Knowledge Graph, Transfer, Synthesis, project direction, or production authority.

**Canonical contract:** `modules/creative-knowledge-generalist-v1/charter.mjs`

## Purpose

`creative-knowledge-generalist-v1` will provide a durable cross-domain creative reasoning substrate that AI Studio OS can retrieve from for project-specific Transfer and Synthesis without treating knowledge as creative authority. It reuses the existing Creative Intelligence Foundation, Creative Knowledge Graph, and provenance-backed retrieval contracts.

Its constitutional boundary is explicit: knowledge is not authority; a reference is not direction; a pattern is not a solution; a trend is not justification; technology is not concept; and a critic score is not selection.

## Frozen Coverage

The populated V1 corpus will contain exactly three qualified causal principles in each of these eight domains, for 24 entries total:

1. `editorial-and-information-design`
2. `architecture-and-spatial-experience`
3. `industrial-and-product-design`
4. `film-and-cinematography`
5. `photography-and-visual-framing`
6. `music-and-rhythm`
7. `interaction-and-hci`
8. `visual-perception-and-cognition`

Each entry will use `ai-studio-os/creative-knowledge-entry@1`, `kind: principle`, and `scope: general`. It must meet the Foundation entry contract and include a full Transfer decomposition: transferable principle, surface signature, material to strip, adaptation rules, and copy risks. The entry is a bounded causal claim, not a style recipe, famous reference, technology trick, inspiration board, or generic maxim.

## Source And Provenance Protocol

Population will prioritize peer-reviewed research, academic or institutional publications, authoritative standards or documented theory, identifiable books/texts with a locator, and attributable first-party work by recognized practitioners or institutions. Each domain requires at least two independent source identities; one source may not dominate the domain.

SEO blogs, listicles, marketing pages, unsourced Medium posts, AI summaries, reference aggregators, current benchmark artifacts, AI Studio OS fixtures/tests, and earlier generated dogfood knowledge are forbidden as primary evidence. Every source record must carry a stable identity, type, externally resolvable reference where possible, title, author or organization, publication date where known, capture date, locator, and concise provenance note. Large copied passages are not stored; principles are paraphrased. `internal://` references are prohibited for production-corpus provenance.

Confidence is evidence-bound: `0.90` requires multiple strong independent sources and a tightly bounded claim; `0.80` requires strong primary/authoritative evidence plus support and named context dependency; `0.70` permits credible documented evidence with wider uncertainty. Below `0.70` is excluded, and no entry receives `1.0` certainty.

## Diversity And Neutrality

The three principles in each domain must describe distinct causal mechanisms. The corpus must also resist rephrasing one universal idea across its domains. Relationships may express reinforcement, conflict, dependency, qualification, counterexample, or derivation where that helps retrieval and review.

Corpus population is isolated from active project demands. It must not select, word, tag, or rank entries from Benchmark 011, After Matter, Friction Index, Condition D, current Motion hypotheses, or expected benchmark scoring. In particular, corpus entries cannot contain `benchmark-011`, `after-matter`, or `friction-index` project references.

No Generalist V1 knowledge ID is preselected for Benchmark 011 or another project. The charter creates no benchmark eligibility flag and no project-specific eligibility mapping. Only a later project-specific retrieval may select from the completed frozen corpus using the existing graph and retrieval contracts.

## Experiment Chronology

Generalist Corpus V1 is pre-registered after Benchmark 011, After Matter, and the selected Friction Index Creative World were already known. Its contamination firewall prevents deliberate project targeting during population, but it does not recreate researcher or author blindness to that benchmark.

Once populated and frozen, Benchmark 011 may resume as post-target developmental evidence: pipeline integration, real Condition D execution, qualitative architecture behavior, harness integrity, and downstream proof/review flow. It is not clean benchmark-blind confirmatory evidence of a Generalist V1 or Condition D capability advantage. Any capability interpretation must disclose this post-target corpus history.

The first clean confirmatory evaluation must use a future benchmark whose project brief, Creative Thesis, and Creative World were unavailable during corpus population. The corpus must be frozen before that unseen benchmark is revealed, and the architecture must remain frozen through that experiment.

## Population And Freeze

After this charter is merged, a separate population PR must research sources, build a source manifest, author exactly 24 entries, build/review the Foundation and Knowledge Graph, freeze their fingerprints, test provenance and retrieval neutrality, then merge before any Benchmark 011 post-target developmental evaluation resumes. The corpus must instead freeze before any future unseen confirmatory benchmark is revealed. Difficulty finding qualified material in a registered domain requires a separate charter revision; it does not permit a silent change to domains, entry count, or source rules.

The completed corpus will expose its ID and version, entry and domain counts, entry IDs, source-manifest/library/Foundation/graph fingerprints, timestamps, and truth that it is general-purpose, not benchmark-tailored, and carries no project, creative, or production authority. Any later content change is a new version or explicit corpus revision.
