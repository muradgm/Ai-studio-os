# brand-fit-review

## Purpose
Check whether an artifact actually behaves like the approved brand rather than merely looking polished or consistent with a moodboard.

## When to use
- Identity, website, campaign, copy, image, motion, or product work that represents the brand publicly.
- Before approving a major direction or final delivery.

## Inputs required
- Approved brand strategy, positioning, behaviors, anti-behaviors, and reasons to believe.
- Creative direction and dials.
- Artifact in context.
- Relevant category/competitor references.

## Operating principles
- Brand fit is behavioral, not just color/font compliance.
- Test whether the artifact reinforces the chosen strategic tension and audience value.
- Consistency is not enough if the system is consistently generic.
- Do not reject useful category codes merely to appear unique.
- Do not let a fashionable visual direction rewrite the brand strategy downstream.
- Separate brand mismatch from craft defects that belong to other reviewers.

## Workflow
1. Restate the brand's positioning and 3–5 observable behaviors.
2. Cold-read the artifact: what personality/value/category does it imply?
3. Compare implied behavior to the approved brand behavior.
4. Check whether copy, typography, composition, imagery, motion, interaction, and proof reinforce or contradict each other.
5. Identify where the work has drifted toward category default or reference imitation.
6. Classify mismatch severity by impact on recognition, trust, and strategic differentiation.
7. Return APPROVE / REVISE / REJECT with a preserve list and corrections.

## Deliverables
- Cold-read brand perception.
- Fit/mismatch matrix.
- Category-default and drift findings.
- Preserve list.
- Severity-ranked corrections.
- Decision.

## Review criteria
- Artifact communicates the intended value/behavior without relying on explanation.
- Brand character is consistent across relevant media.
- Distinctive choices are strategically grounded.
- Category comprehension remains intact.
- Proof and product behavior support the brand promise.

## Failure modes
- Checking only logo/color/font consistency.
- Treating brand guidelines as immutable when strategy has explicitly changed.
- Calling every unconventional choice “off-brand.”
- Confusing personal taste with strategic fit.
- Ignoring contradictions between copy and visual behavior.

## Handoffs
- `brand-strategist` resolves upstream positioning ambiguity.
- `art-direction` resolves visual-system drift.
- `copywriter`, `logo-designer`, `motion-designer`, `image-director`, or `product-designer` revise their own craft.
- Council handles consequential unresolved disagreement.
