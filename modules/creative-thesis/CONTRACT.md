# Creative Thesis contract

## Purpose

Turn validated project truth and inspiration into **one governing creative idea** before visual direction, layout, motion, 3D, or implementation choices begin.

A Creative Thesis is not a moodboard, aesthetic label, layout, technology choice, slogan, or collection of references. It explains **why this experience should exist in this particular form for this particular project**.

## Required inputs

- project intent / problem
- business or product truths that cannot be violated
- inspiration opportunity gaps and anti-references
- at least two useful creative traits or tensions when available
- explicit anti-principles / category defaults to reject
- unresolved unknowns preserved as unresolved
- audience and commercial objective when known; absence must remain visible rather than invented

## Required output

- one governing idea
- one creative tension
- why the idea belongs to this project
- source truth references
- source opportunity reference
- category conventions explicitly rejected
- creative principles and anti-principles
- expression tests for typography, image, motion, interaction, sound, and responsive behavior
- technology-neutral implementation policy
- competitor-transfer test
- unresolved risks / unknowns
- review findings and approval state

## Gates

A thesis cannot be considered review-ready unless:

1. **Truth anchored** — it references real project/business truth instead of category assumptions.
2. **Opportunity anchored** — it exploits a documented opportunity gap rather than generating a style from nowhere.
3. **Singular** — one governing idea exists. Multiple unrelated ideas must be resolved before art direction.
4. **Ownable** — a direct competitor should not be able to reuse the thesis unchanged without losing the source truth that makes it meaningful.
5. **Anti-generic** — category defaults and anti-references are carried forward explicitly.
6. **Cross-modal** — the idea can influence type, image, motion, interaction, sound when justified, and responsive behavior without becoming a visual gimmick.
7. **Technology neutral** — Three.js, WebGL, GSAP, Rive, AI image generation, video, 3D, or any other tool may serve the idea but may not *be* the idea.
8. **Truthful uncertainty** — missing research, audience, objective, rights, or product information remains visible.

## Approval semantics

- `blocked` — a required truth/intent foundation is missing.
- `provisional` — a coherent draft exists but major evidence or specificity gaps remain.
- `ready-for-creative-direction-review` — the contract passes structural review; this is **not human creative approval**.
- `approved` / `frozen` must never be inferred by this runtime.

## Failure modes

- “premium, modern, immersive” presented as a thesis
- combining references by averaging their visual styles
- treating WebGL, scroll animation, 3D, shaders, gradients, or large typography as the concept
- generic luxury / tech / hospitality language that could be pasted onto a competitor
- invented audience insight or commercial proof
- multiple competing ideas hidden inside one paragraph
- motion, imagery, or interaction added without a relationship to the governing idea

## Handoff

A reviewed Creative Thesis feeds Creative Direction and later Creative World exploration. Downstream art direction may transform its expression, but it may not silently replace the governing idea or violate its truth anchors.
