# observation-runtime

## Procedure
1. Identify the shipped change, hypothesis, primary metrics, and guardrails.
2. Collect outcome evidence with source, sample, window, and baseline/observed values.
3. Analyze only metrics that meet their evidence thresholds.
4. Synthesize qualitative feedback without treating unsupported anecdotes as data.
5. Review the launch as success, mixed, regression, or insufficient evidence.
6. Check benchmark history for active regressions.
7. Promote only learnings that meet scope-appropriate evidence thresholds; preserve holds and rejections.
