# Creative Calibration Contract

**Input:** evidenced references, Design Read, ten rationalized Creative Dials, 3–5 concept candidates, and an explicit selection decision.

**Output:** transformed reference system, compact business/design interpretation, dial fingerprint, genuinely divergent concepts, selected concept, rejected alternatives, and kill criteria.

**Block:** copying intent, weak/missing reference evidence, incomplete Design Read, invalid/rationale-free dials, cosmetic concept variants, or unexplained convergence.
