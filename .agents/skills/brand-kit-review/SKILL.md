# brand-kit-review

## Purpose
Decide whether a multi-asset brand identity is genuinely coherent and deliverable rather than merely visually related or administratively complete.

## When to use
- Before a full identity kit is marked ready for delivery.
- After logo, icon, type, color, guidelines and representative applications have been produced.

## Inputs required
- Brand Kit manifest.
- Approved Brand DNA.
- Required artifact references.
- Logo/vector review and integrity evidence.
- Rights/provenance metadata.
- Representative application tests.

## Operating principles
- Reviewer must be independent from the makers whose artifacts are being certified.
- File count is not a quality signal.
- One Brand DNA version must govern the delivered system.
- Personalized icons must prove a constructed family, not a recolored stock library.
- Legal and licensing evidence is reported, not assumed.
- A missing required artifact is a blocker, not a cosmetic note.

## Workflow
1. Confirm Brand DNA exists, is versioned, and contains strategy plus governing creative direction.
2. Verify every required artifact exists, is approved/frozen, and references the same Brand DNA version.
3. Check logo review and Logo Integrity evidence.
4. Check personalized icon system for Icon DNA, calibration family, actual SVG masters and independent vector review.
5. Review typography licensing records and reject unauthorized font redistribution.
6. Inspect representative applications for real cross-medium coherence.
7. Review brand-fit evidence and unresolved risks.
8. Return `ready`, `review`, or `blocked` with actionable findings.

## Deliverables
- Brand Kit verdict.
- Blocking and major findings.
- Coherence/version-drift findings.
- Personalized-icon verification.
- Rights/legal risk notes.
- Delivery recommendation.

## Review criteria
- Strategy, verbal identity and visual system express one governing idea.
- Required artifacts are real, approved and version-consistent.
- Logo and icon systems retain independent integrity.
- Guidelines describe usable rules rather than a decorative presentation.
- Applications demonstrate the system under realistic constraints.

## Failure modes
- Reviewer participated as the maker of the final kit.
- Approving because individual assets look attractive in isolation.
- Ignoring version drift between logo, icons and applications.
- Treating stock icons as personalized simply because colors changed.
- Treating missing legal evidence as clearance.

## Handoffs
- Passed kits may move to delivery/export packaging.
- Blocked/review findings route back only to the responsible specialist or asset rather than regenerating the whole identity.
