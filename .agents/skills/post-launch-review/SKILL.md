# post-launch-review

## Procedure
1. Load analytics and reliable feedback themes.
2. Check whether enough primary evidence exists to make a decision.
3. Block on guardrail regression.
4. Classify the outcome as success, mixed, regression, or insufficient-evidence.
5. Preserve whether the evidence is correlational or causal-supported.
6. Separate product outcome from process/runtime health.
