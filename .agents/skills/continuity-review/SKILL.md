# continuity-review

## Procedure
1. Load the continuity bible and identify required versus locked fields.
2. Inspect every shot for missing continuity metadata.
3. Compare locked values across all shots.
4. Distinguish intentional scene variation from accidental drift.
5. Block factual/product/character continuity failures.
6. Preserve the same continuity decisions through video, motion, and generated assets.
