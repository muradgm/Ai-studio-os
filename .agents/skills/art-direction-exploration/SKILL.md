# art-direction-exploration

## Purpose
Turn a selected experience thesis into several materially different visual and interaction worlds while preserving the locked experience behavior.

## When to use
- after a Creative World / experience thesis has explicit human selection;
- when the selected idea is stronger than its current visual treatment;
- before typography approval, production technology selection, or full-site build.

## Inputs required
- one confirmed experience-thesis lock;
- 3–5 authored art-direction candidates;
- project truth and asset constraints relevant to visual proof.

## Operating principles
- Lock behavior, free form.
- Do not preserve weak visual motifs merely because they appeared in an earlier proof.
- Divergence must be formal and experiential, not palette variation.
- Typography remains behavior/intent until visual specimens are reviewed.
- Technology remains downstream.
- Real products and documentary claims require real source/capture evidence.
- Mobile may reinterpret the art direction but must preserve the locked service sequence.
- No automated score may select the art direction.

## Workflow
1. Restate the locked experience thesis and sequence.
2. Explicitly list what the art director may discard from previous visual proof.
3. Author 3–5 art directions with different spatial, compositional, typographic, image, material, motion and interaction models.
4. Reject candidates that differ across fewer than five formal dimensions.
5. Produce the same representative moments for each candidate.
6. Compare opening, sensory/product, utility, transition and mobile proof.
7. Record strengths, risks and what would need to improve.
8. Ask for human art-direction selection or rejection.

## Deliverables
- experience thesis lock;
- art-direction exploration manifest;
- 3–5 candidate direction specs;
- browser-rendered visual proof for each direction;
- same-moment comparison boards;
- mobile proof;
- truth manifest with visual approval and production readiness still false.

## Review criteria
- selected experience behavior is unchanged;
- candidates differ structurally rather than cosmetically;
- at least one candidate is not dependent on the prior visual grammar;
- practical utility content survives the direction;
- mobile is a first-class interpretation;
- typography is not prematurely canonicalized;
- no candidate fabricates product imagery or human approval.

## Failure modes
- keeping the old palette and changing only layout;
- using the same serif/sans pairing in every candidate;
- converting the experience thesis into a literal visual gimmick;
- treating WebGL, 3D, Rive or GSAP as the art direction;
- merging all candidates into a safe hybrid before review;
- automatically selecting the easiest direction to implement.

## Handoffs
- `creative-world-exploration` provides the selected experience thesis;
- `art-direction` authors the formal interpretations;
- `creative-critic` and `creative-skeptic` review visual ownership and transfer risk;
- Typography Intelligence remains downstream of selected art direction and visual proof;
- Creative Engineering begins only after art direction and later motion/interaction language are approved.
