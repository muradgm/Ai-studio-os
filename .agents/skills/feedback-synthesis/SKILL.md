# feedback-synthesis

## Procedure
1. Preserve source type, count, evidence, sentiment, and criticality.
2. Group repeated observations by theme.
3. Exclude unsupported or malformed entries from reliable themes.
4. Keep contradictory themes visible.
5. Treat critical negative feedback separately from ordinary taste disagreement.
6. Feed reliable themes into post-launch review and learning promotion.
