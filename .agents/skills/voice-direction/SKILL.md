# voice-direction

## Procedure
1. Separate writing voice from audio performance direction.
2. Define language, cast traits, tempo, energy, pauses, and pronunciation.
3. Map every line to a valid storyboard beat and keep it inside that beat timing.
4. Require usage-rights evidence for every voice source.
5. Require explicit consent evidence for voice cloning.
6. Keep narration density low enough for image, sound, and silence to work.
