# storyboard-direction

## Procedure
1. Define the narrative beats and their exact durations.
2. Map every shot to one beat with unique IDs and explicit purpose.
3. Define camera intent, source policy, truth sensitivity, and transition intent.
4. Require evidence for real-source truth-sensitive shots.
5. Ensure shot duration sums match both beat durations and total runtime.
6. Flag capture-required shots as pending rather than pretending production is ready.
