# Benchmark 001 — Du Bonheur

A real French pâtisserie/café in Berlin used as the first creative benchmark for AI Studio OS.

## Benchmark purpose
Test whether the kernel routes a real brand/web task through research, inspiration, creative direction, image strategy, motion, critique, evaluation, and learning without defaulting to generic AI luxury design.

## Success questions
- Does the workflow uncover real business truth before art direction?
- Does inspiration include adjacent industries and anti-references?
- Does image strategy prioritize real assets and retouching?
- Does motion emerge from brand/texture rather than generic scroll effects?
- Does critique distinguish defects from taste?
