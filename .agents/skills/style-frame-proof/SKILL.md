# style-frame-proof

## Purpose
Prove whether 3–5 Creative Worlds survive real composition at the same product moments before a winner is selected.

## When to use
- after Creative World Exploration is structurally review-ready;
- before human world selection;
- before final typography, motion choreography, or production technology;
- whenever prose-level art directions could hide that the options are visually generic or too similar.

## Inputs required
- review-ready Creative World Exploration;
- 4–7 project-specific proof moments;
- product truth and documentary-asset constraints;
- responsive/mobile requirements.

## Operating principles
- Compare like with like: every world gets the same proof moments.
- Browser output must be the authored composition, not an image-model reinterpretation.
- Use proxy/system typography until a world is selected.
- Real documentary claims require real sources; placeholders must be explicit.
- A beautiful frame does not override product truth.
- A structural pass does not imply a creative pass.
- Do not auto-select the winner.
- Mobile is part of the world, not a later adaptation.

## Workflow
1. Read the selected project Product Understanding, Creative Thesis, and all candidate worlds.
2. Define 4–7 proof moments that exercise the actual product mechanics.
3. Ensure all worlds receive identical moment coverage.
4. Author each frame from the world’s narrative, composition, type intent, imagery, material, interaction, motion, and responsive logic.
5. Render exact HTML/CSS/SVG in Chromium.
6. Save source HTML and exact browser raster for every frame.
7. Build same-moment comparison boards and per-world overview boards.
8. Produce a proof manifest with evidence refs and truth boundaries.
9. Run independent creative review for genericity, category transfer, product fit, hierarchy, and meaningful divergence.
10. Hand all viable proof to the human/creative director. Selection remains null until that review happens.

## Deliverables
- Style Frame Proof plan;
- exact rendered frames;
- matching source HTML;
- same-moment comparisons;
- per-world overviews;
- evidence manifest;
- unresolved risks;
- no selected world.

## Review criteria
- every world proves the same product states;
- worlds remain clearly different after real composition;
- the Creative Thesis is visible without becoming a repeated motif;
- product mechanics remain legible;
- no generic AI/SaaS shortcut replaces project truth;
- mobile preserves the world;
- truth and approval states remain honest.

## Failure modes
- one world gets stronger/more useful proof moments than the others;
- frames are just palette/type variants;
- 3D, glow, WebGL, or motion becomes the idea;
- agent portraits/nodes appear because the product contains agents;
- typography is treated as approved before selection;
- browser frames use fabricated documentary evidence;
- the runtime or a numerical score chooses the winner;
- desktop looks distinct but mobile collapses into generic cards/chat.

## Handoffs
- `creative-world-exploration` supplies candidate worlds.
- `art-direction` authors project-specific visual expression.
- `creative-skeptic` challenges genericity and transfer risk.
- human visual review selects/rejects/patches worlds after proof.
- Typography Intelligence and Creative Direction consume only the selected world.
