# brand-identity-recipe

## Purpose
Orchestrate a complete brand-identity project without collapsing strategy, design, and review into one generation step.

## When to use
- New brand identity or major rebrand.
- When the deliverable includes multiple identity components rather than a logo alone.

## Inputs required
- Business/product truth.
- Audience and category context.
- Existing brand/name/assets if any.
- Required applications and launch scope.
- Legal/trademark constraints where relevant.

## Operating principles
- Business/positioning precedes art direction.
- Inspiration maps territory; it does not authorize copying.
- Diverge before selecting a direction.
- Logo is one component of the identity, not the whole identity.
- Maker and reviewer remain independent.
- Do not freeze canonical assets before explicit approval.

## Workflow
1. `brand-strategist` — positioning, audience job, category tension, behaviors, risks.
2. Inspiration/reference extraction — TAKE / REJECT / TRANSFORM plus anti-references.
3. `art-direction` — creative tension, visual language, principles, dials.
4. Explore identity territories; use `creative-skeptic` for high-risk selection.
5. `logo-designer` + `logo-exploration` — type assessment, concept families, finalist.
6. `logo-review` + `brand-fit-review` — independent gate.
7. Build typography, color, image, layout, graphic, and motion systems from the same direction.
8. Test responsive identity and representative real-world applications.
9. Freeze approved vector/logo assets through Logo Integrity.
10. Package identity rules, assets, rationale, usage constraints, and unresolved legal/commercial risks.

## Deliverables
- Brand strategy and positioning.
- Creative direction.
- Identity territory decision.
- Logo system and responsive marks.
- Typography/color/image/graphic/motion rules as required.
- Representative applications.
- Independent review verdicts.
- Final asset/version package and handoff notes.

## Review criteria
- One coherent strategic idea is visible across media.
- Identity is distinctive without losing category comprehension.
- Logo and wider system work independently and together.
- Rules are usable in real production, not just presentation boards.
- Legal/trademark uncertainty is not falsely represented as cleared.

## Failure modes
- Starting with logo sketches before positioning.
- Treating a palette/typeface/logo board as a complete brand.
- Copying one reference aesthetic across every touchpoint.
- Overbuilding guidelines before the identity is tested in applications.
- Maker approving its own final work.

## Handoffs
- Approved identity becomes shared context for product, web, campaign, image, motion, and copy recipes.
- Observation/learning may update rules only from validated recurring evidence.
