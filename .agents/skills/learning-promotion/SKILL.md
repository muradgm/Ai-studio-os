# learning-promotion

## Procedure
1. Require an explicit candidate rule and scope.
2. Gather reliable supporting and conflicting metrics/feedback and preserve their evidence-source types.
3. Reject when reliable conflicting evidence exists.
4. Hold when outcome evidence is insufficient or support is too weak.
5. Allow project-rule promotion only when the minimum number of independent evidence sources is met in one project.
6. Require global rules to recur with evidence across at least two distinct contexts.
7. Record promoted, held, and rejected candidates separately.
