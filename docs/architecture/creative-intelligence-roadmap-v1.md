# Creative Intelligence roadmap V1

## Status

Locked sequencing decision for AI Studio OS after Motion Creative Intelligence V1.

This roadmap does **not** replace the canonical creative authority architecture already established on `main`. It strengthens the intelligence and knowledge substrate that feeds Creative Thesis, Creative World, Drawing Intelligence, Motion Intelligence and later Spatial Creative Intelligence.

## Core decision

Do not stop or rewrite the existing system.

Finish and validate Motion Creative Intelligence V1 first. Then insert a shared Creative Intelligence foundation **before** broad expansion into Spatial / 3D / physics / shader production intelligence.

Canonical build order:

```text
1. Finish Motion Creative Intelligence V1
2. Exact-head CI + adversarial review + merge
3. Creative Intelligence Foundation V1
4. Creative Knowledge representation / graph
5. Creative Transfer Intelligence
6. Creative Synthesis Intelligence
7. Motion Intelligence V2 / deep motion knowledge
8. Serious motion benchmarks / dogfood
9. Spatial Creative Intelligence V1
10. Camera + cinematography intelligence
11. Geometry / transforms
12. Material / shader intelligence
13. Physical-behavior intelligence
14. Blender / browser production intelligence
15. Advanced simulation only when real evidence requires it
```

## What remains authoritative

Keep and extend rather than replace:

- Product Understanding
- Product UX Architecture
- Creative Thesis deliberation and human thesis authority
- Creative World exploration / visual proof / human selection
- Creative Direction
- Drawing Intelligence -> Geometry Intent -> Vector Geometry
- Typography Intelligence
- Creative Quality / Calibration
- Motion Creative Intelligence authority / proof / Critic / human selection
- fail-closed production boundaries

The existing authority architecture is a strength. The next problem is the depth of the intelligence flowing through it.

## Architectural distinction

AI Studio OS must preserve three separate concepts:

```text
KNOWLEDGE
What Studio knows.

INTELLIGENCE
How Studio reasons with that knowledge.

CRAFT
How Studio constructs and executes the result.
```

Knowledge is advisory evidence. It is never authority by itself.

## Constitutional rules

```text
KNOWLEDGE != AUTHORITY
REFERENCE != DIRECTION
PATTERN != SOLUTION
TREND != JUSTIFICATION
TECHNOLOGY != CONCEPT
CRITIC SCORE != SELECTION
```

Human authority and existing canonical proof gates remain downstream where already required.

## Creative Intelligence Foundation V1

This should be a shared reasoning substrate, not another bureaucratic stage every project must visibly traverse.

Target domains:

- foundational design theory
- perception / visual cognition
- composition and hierarchy
- art direction
- typography
- color
- spatial reasoning
- image / photography / compositing
- drawing / shape / symbolic reduction
- interaction
- motion
- brand
- historical / cultural / cross-disciplinary references
- creative judgment and critique

Target reasoning capabilities:

- causal creative reasoning
- analogy
- abstraction
- contradiction reasoning
- project-grounded inference
- appropriateness judgment
- genericity detection
- originality / ownability reasoning
- creative transfer
- creative synthesis
- critique and refinement

Do not create one runtime module per taxonomy item unless execution evidence justifies it. Conceptual richness must not become architecture theater.

## Creative Knowledge representation

Knowledge entries should encode more than labels or example prompts.

Minimum conceptual model:

```text
principle
meaning / definition
why it works
perceptual effect
conditions where it works
conditions where it fails
relationships / dependencies / conflicts
creative variables
cross-domain applications
adaptation rules
failure modes
counterexamples
diagnostics
provenance
confidence
scope
transferability
```

Knowledge must distinguish at least:

- general principle
- historical / cultural precedent
- current trend
- project-specific observation
- benchmark-derived learning
- human preference / approval
- uncertain inference

A large unqualified library of opinions is not Creative Intelligence.

## Creative Transfer Intelligence

Studio should learn transferable principles from references without surface imitation.

```text
reference
-> decompose
-> identify causal creative principles
-> strip surface signature
-> evaluate relevance to current project
-> transform
-> combine with product / brand truth
-> generate new hypothesis
```

Relevant source domains may include architecture, cinema, graphic design, fashion, industrial design, photography, painting, sculpture, title sequences, installation art, music videos, editorial design, physical materials and natural systems.

The goal is not style copying. The goal is principled transfer.

## Creative Synthesis Intelligence

Studio must be able to combine several influences into one coherent creative language rather than a collage.

It should reason about:

- which principles reinforce one another
- which principles conflict
- dominance and hierarchy between influences
- what should remain subtle
- what should be removed
- whether the resulting system still belongs to the project
- whether the synthesis is ownable and coherent

## Relationship to Creative Thesis

Do not replace Creative Thesis authority.

Future thesis deliberation should be strengthened by:

```text
project truth
+ research
+ Creative Knowledge
+ transferable principles
+ cultural / contextual knowledge
-> contradictions
-> materially different hypotheses
-> adversarial critique
-> transfer / synthesis where justified
-> governing Creative Thesis
-> existing human authority gate
```

- color behavior

- spatial philosophy

## Relationship to Drawing Intelligence

Keep the Brain / Hand boundary:

```text
semantic + visual knowledge
-> Drawing Intelligence
-> semantic drawing hypothesis
-> Geometry Intent
-> Vector Geometry
-> render proof
-> review / human authority where required
```

Creative Knowledge should improve semantic judgment, metaphor choice, visual-vocabulary collision reasoning, proportion / perceptual judgment and authored family logic. It must not bypass exact geometry authority.

## Motion Intelligence V2 direction

Motion Creative Intelligence V1 establishes the authority, exploration, temporal proof, Critic and selection skeleton.

V2 should deepen the brain rather than replace that skeleton.

Target reasoning domains:

- motion semantics
- attention choreography
- temporal composition
- narrative timing
- motion hierarchy
- rhythm and pacing
- anticipation, overlap, holds and stillness
- perceived mass / inertia / friction / elasticity / damping
- spatial choreography
- interaction choreography
- scroll choreography
- cinematic language
- camera / framing / reveal / continuity
- editing language: cuts, matches, transitions, beats, temporal compression / expansion
- typographic motion
- image motion
- material motion
- procedural / generative motion
- responsive reinterpretation
- reduced-motion equivalence
- accessibility
- performance reasoning
- motion critique

Motion patterns / examples are evidence beneath this system, not the authority itself.

Before selecting a construct, Motion Intelligence should be able to answer:

```text
Why should anything move?
What does this movement communicate?
Where should attention be now and next?
What remains still?
What physical character belongs to this world?
What temporal hierarchy serves the product meaning?
How does mobile reinterpret the behavior?
What is the reduced-motion semantic equivalent?
Is the effect worth its complexity and render cost?
```

## Relationship to Spatial Creative Intelligence

The existing Spatial Creative Intelligence direction remains valid, especially the rule that Three.js, WebGL/WebGPU, shaders, Blender and physics engines are production technologies rather than creative concepts.

The sequencing changes: Spatial expansion follows Creative Intelligence Foundation, Transfer / Synthesis and deeper Motion Intelligence rather than immediately following Motion V1.

Spatial should later own:

- spatial necessity
- composition in depth
- camera intelligence
- geometry / transform reasoning
- material / shader intent
- physical behavior
- Blender vs live-browser strategy
- production feasibility / budgets / fallbacks

## Benchmarks

Do not measure progress only through schema completeness.

Use before / after dogfood benchmarks that ask whether deeper intelligence materially improves creative output.

Good candidates include:

- AI Council Motion System: can Motion Intelligence V2 independently produce a stronger project-specific direction than the existing approved V1?
- Standard UI Icon Logic: does Creative Intelligence materially improve Drawing Intelligence while preserving recognition and restraint?
- AI Council semantic icons: can transfer / synthesis produce more ownable metaphors without learned-symbol collisions?
- animated gradient / background system: can Studio derive distinctive motion / material systems instead of retrieving fashionable effects?
- real landing-page / product experience: can Studio explain why the final design and motion work, not merely reproduce a reference?

## Anti-goals

Do not build:

- a prompt gallery and call it Creative Knowledge
- a Motion recipe library and call it Motion Intelligence
- reference cloning
- a giant taxonomy with no executable reasoning
- trend retrieval as design authority
- generic Awwwards aesthetics as a quality target by themselves
- WebGL / 3D / shaders as proof of sophistication
- autonomous creative approval from scores or retrieved precedent

## Current execution gate

Do not start Creative Intelligence Foundation implementation until Motion Creative Intelligence V1 has:

- exact-head CI evidence
- exact-head adversarial review
- all blocking findings resolved
- merge authority satisfied

Once Motion V1 is merged, Creative Intelligence Foundation V1 becomes the next implementation branch before Spatial Creative Intelligence V1.
