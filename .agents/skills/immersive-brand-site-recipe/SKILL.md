# Immersive Brand Site Recipe

## Purpose
Coordinate an authored, technically ambitious brand experience without letting realtime spectacle replace business/content clarity.

## When to use
Use when the approved concept genuinely benefits from spatial graphics, strong motion, unusual interaction, or cinematic transitions beyond a standard landing-page build.

## Inputs required
Validated brief, audience/business objective, approved creative direction, content, asset rights, interaction concept, target devices, technical constraints, performance budget, and delivery requirements.

## Operating principles
- Research and creative direction precede runtime choice.
- The experience must work as a coherent semantic/static baseline.
- Realtime graphics are a production lane, not the site architecture.
- Mobile/reduced-motion/low-performance variants are part of the concept.
- Browser evidence and independent review are mandatory before delivery.
- Provider/tool choices stay replaceable.

## Workflow
1. Brief/research/inspiration and concept selection.
2. Art direction + product/content hierarchy.
3. Interaction and motion specification.
4. Creative Engineering Plan and budgets.
5. Semantic/static frontend baseline.
6. 3D asset preparation and realtime scene construction only where justified.
7. Motion engineering and responsive immersive adaptation.
8. Playwright capture across required viewports and reduced-motion.
9. Creative Development + WebGL + Responsive Motion reviews.
10. Performance + Accessibility delivery gates.
11. Capped patch loop and recapture.
12. Release only when blockers/majors satisfy the project gate.

## Deliverables
Runnable website, source, asset/provenance registry, browser captures, responsive/reduced-motion variants, performance evidence, review verdicts, patch history, and delivery package.

## Review criteria
The result is distinctive, content/product meaning remains clear, realtime work earns its cost, required devices are authored, and independent delivery gates pass.

## Failure modes
Awwwards cosplay, full-screen canvas with weak content, cinematic intro before value, desktop-only art direction, inaccessible GPU-only meaning, performance budgets created after implementation, or shipping visual polish with unresolved major engineering findings.

## Handoffs
Composes Art Direction, Product/Copy/Motion design with Creative Developer, Realtime WebGL Engineer, Motion Engineer, 3D Technical Artist, task skills, and independent creative/performance/accessibility/responsive reviews.
