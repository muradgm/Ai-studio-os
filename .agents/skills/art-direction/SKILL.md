# art-direction

## Procedure
1. Load product truth, intent, inspiration packet, and anti-references.
2. Name the dominant creative tension (for example craft × restraint).
3. Select one direction; do not average incompatible references.
4. Define non-negotiables, principles, anti-principles, and implications for design/image/motion/writing.
5. Record unresolved risks and what evidence could change the direction.
6. Hand off to design, image, and motion with the same direction statement.
