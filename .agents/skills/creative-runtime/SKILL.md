# creative-runtime

## Procedure
1. Confirm intent and business truths.
2. Build/verify the inspiration packet including anti-references and opportunity gaps.
3. Synthesize one creative direction.
4. Produce design, image, and motion work packets.
5. Preserve unresolved unknowns rather than inventing facts.
6. Prepare the creative eval rubric before final artifact review.
7. For benchmark fixtures, run the deterministic CLI benchmark and report failures.
