# video-direction

## Procedure
1. Inherit the approved creative direction and storyboard without rewriting their intent.
2. Preserve shot IDs, beat mapping, source policy, and continuity metadata.
3. Define camera/edit decisions that change attention for a reason.
4. Create explicit recomposition/safe-area plans for vertical formats.
5. Avoid decorative movement, gratuitous speed ramps, and blind aspect-ratio crops.
6. Send the complete plan to video-review and multimodal-review.
