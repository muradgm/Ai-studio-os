# video-review

## Procedure
1. Compare every shot against storyboard purpose and timing.
2. Check camera movement and transitions for narrative purpose.
3. Check real-subject source evidence and physical plausibility.
4. Check continuity locks and format-specific recomposition.
5. Separate blockers from taste notes.
6. Return approve, revise, or reject with prioritized corrections.
