# multimodal-review

## Procedure
1. Compare every discipline against the same creative-direction statement and traits.
2. Block continuity drift, timing mismatch, truth/source failures, unresolved rights, and orphan cues.
3. Check narration density and whether audio leaves room for visual storytelling.
4. Check captions/transcript for spoken content.
5. Check vertical-format adaptation rather than assuming a master crop works everywhere.
6. Do not approve individually good media layers that feel like different projects.
