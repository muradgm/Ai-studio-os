# Cinematic Product Page Recipe

## Purpose
Create a visually memorable product narrative without allowing cinematic devices to obscure the product, truth boundary, or task flow.

## When to use
Use for launch pages, premium product storytelling, technical-product showcases, and hero-to-interface experiences where motion/3D communicates the product idea.

## Inputs required
Verified product truth, approved positioning, product UI/evidence, creative direction, motion concept, asset plan, viewport matrix, reduced-motion plan, performance budget, and release constraints.

## Operating principles
- Show the product early enough to establish credibility.
- Cinematic motion must explain product state, transformation, material, or context.
- Never fabricate product UI or performance proof without explicit prototype labeling.
- One strong signature sequence is better than unrelated effects in every section.
- The product surface becomes flatter and clearer as the narrative approaches action.

## Workflow
1. Verify truth boundary and product evidence.
2. Select one narrative transformation.
3. Build content/product hierarchy and static baseline.
4. Specify motion states and signature sequence.
5. Create Creative Engineering Plan.
6. Implement motion/realtime lanes only where justified.
7. Adapt mobile/reduced-motion behavior.
8. Capture actual browser output.
9. Run creative development, responsive motion, performance, and accessibility reviews.
10. Patch, recapture, and deliver.

## Deliverables
Runnable page, product evidence surfaces, signature motion implementation, responsive/reduced-motion states, browser captures, performance evidence, and review history.

## Review criteria
The product remains believable and understandable, cinematic behavior has explanatory purpose, the signature sequence resolves cleanly, and delivery gates pass without invented claims.

## Failure modes
Long intros, fake dashboards, motion as a substitute for product proof, generic metallic 3D, every section behaving differently, mobile reduced to screenshots, or performance exceptions justified by launch-page status.

## Handoffs
Composes Brand/Product/Copy/Motion design with Creative Developer and Motion Engineer; add Realtime WebGL Engineer/3D Technical Artist only when the selected concept needs them. Independent reviews own final approval.
