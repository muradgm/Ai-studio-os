# Council Verdict — Project 001

## Recommendation

**ADVANCE TO PROTOTYPE.**

The product concept is coherent enough to build a first working shell. Do not attempt a full agency platform yet. The first prototype should prove one complete project journey rather than surface every runtime.

## Strongest supporting case

The name creates an immediately understandable promise: the product behaves like an agency, not a model picker. The internal AI Studio OS architecture already supports the required specialist stages, evidence gates, Council review, asset integrity, production routing, and learning loop.

## Strongest objection

“The Creative Agency” has weak lexical exclusivity and is already used by active agencies. The product can use it as a working/public-facing name for internal testing, but commercial launch remains blocked on dedicated naming/trademark/domain clearance.

## Product skeptic

Do not confuse process visibility with process theater. If every project requires users to click through ten rooms, the agency becomes bureaucracy. The router must collapse stages when consequence/uncertainty is low.

## Creative director

The Workroom is stronger than a generic dashboard. Keep the visual system editorial and work-led. The output should dominate the screen; the operating system should recede until needed.

## UX/product

Prototype the project spine as the central navigation. Users should always know: what is decided, what remains unresolved, what evidence exists, what is being made, and what needs approval.

## Engineering

The first product slice can remain local/provider-agnostic: project state, stage routing, structured artifacts, Council packets, asset registry, and review status. Do not couple the UI to a specific generation provider.

## Brand/identity

Advance **Bounded Flow v2 — Agency Handoff** to visual exploration, but do not freeze canonical SVG yet. The long descriptive name increases the importance of a proprietary symbol.

## Kill criteria

Stop/revise if the prototype becomes:
- a prompt box with decorative workflow chrome
- a model marketplace
- a rigid 10-step wizard
- a generic dark AI dashboard
- an agency metaphor that hides simple tasks behind unnecessary ceremony
- dependent on the exact “The Creative Agency” name for product comprehension

## Confidence

**0.86 — advance.**
