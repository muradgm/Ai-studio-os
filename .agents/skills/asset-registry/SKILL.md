# asset-registry

## Procedure
1. Give every asset a stable ID and immutable version.
2. Store purpose, direction, continuity, provenance, rights, provider/model, dependencies, review state, output evidence/hash, and cost fields.
3. Patch only validated blocker/major findings tied to an asset ID.
4. Preserve direction, continuity, source relationships, and truth constraints in every patch.
5. Validate versions, review states, and patch counters; stop at the patch-attempt limit and escalate the decision.
